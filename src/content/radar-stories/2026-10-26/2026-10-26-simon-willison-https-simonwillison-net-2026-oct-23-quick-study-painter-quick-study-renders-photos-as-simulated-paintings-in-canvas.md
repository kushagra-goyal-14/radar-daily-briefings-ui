Simon Willison’s Quick Study is a released browser tool that converts photos into simulated paintings. It progressively renders images from coarse brushes to fine ones and supports file-picker, drag-and-drop, and clipboard input.

Users can select oil, watercolor, or pointillist media, choose a detail level, and set a two- to ten-second duration. The experiment uses an HTML canvas and Sobel edge detection, with no external libraries beyond Google Fonts. Users can compare the result with the original, repaint with a new random take, or download a PNG.
