The WordPress Community’s October 2026 newsletter reports student-focused events, meetup growth, contribution activities, and organizer guidance. It presents the roundup as published community news rather than a software release.

A Campus Connect hackathon at Stamford University Bangladesh produced 40 websites and 82 new WordPress.org profiles, but about half of registered students did not participate. WP LATAM’s first online contribution session recorded 20 Photo Directory submissions, 10 translations, and one subtitled video, with 80% of attendees contributing for the first time.

The newsletter also describes a planned move of roughly 700 meetup groups from Meetup.com to events.wordpress.org. Several proposals and guidance updates remain subject to feedback or implementation.
