SterneWidget author fuxdev has requested Project Translation Editor rights for the plugin’s German informal and formal locales, de_DE and de_DE_formal. The request is addressed to the WordPress Polyglots community.

Fuxdev says they are the author of SterneWidget and a native German speaker, and wants access to review and maintain the plugin’s translations. The document identifies the plugin and the requested locale accounts.

The supplied material records only a pending access request. It does not show that the rights were approved or that any translation changes were made.
