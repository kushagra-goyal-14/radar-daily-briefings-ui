The WordPress Community team reports that migration of all community team inboxes from Help Scout to FreeScout is complete. Teams are asked to use FreeScout for community work.

The announcement recommends filtered views, or searching within tags, while work continues to make search faster and more effective. It also explains how to recover conversations from old Help Scout links: extract the conversation ID and append it to FreeScout’s search URL.

Documentation and screenshots have been updated, and users can report missed references. The announcement does not provide architectural details or explain migration validation beyond the operational guidance.
