A contributor has withdrawn a multi-locale WordPress translation review request after feedback about AI-generated translations and the workload it could place on volunteer Polyglots contributors.

The notice says future submissions will focus on languages where quality control and qualified human proofreading can be arranged. It also acknowledges that machine-generated translations should be reviewed by a fluent speaker and follow the relevant locale’s glossary and style guidelines.

The request involved the Krok LLMS Transit plugin translation project. The update documents a withdrawn community-process request, but supplies no detailed proofreading workflow or quality metrics.
