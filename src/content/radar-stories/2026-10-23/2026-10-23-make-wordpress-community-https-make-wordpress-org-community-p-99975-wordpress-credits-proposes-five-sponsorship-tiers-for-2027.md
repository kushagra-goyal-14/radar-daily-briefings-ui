The WordPress Foundation is proposing five sponsorship tiers for the WordPress Credits program, with a pilot planned for 2027. The options range from in-kind mentor sponsorship through Five for the Future to Partner sponsorships starting at $30,000 annually.

The proposed tiers add progressively broader benefits, including partner-page recognition, reports, named program funding, educational posts, and co-hosted events. The Foundation says donated products may be offered to students at no cost and must support exporting or moving student work.

The model remains experimental: the Foundation says it will publish what it learns and adjust or retire elements that do not work. WordPress Campus Connect events remain separate from these tiers.
