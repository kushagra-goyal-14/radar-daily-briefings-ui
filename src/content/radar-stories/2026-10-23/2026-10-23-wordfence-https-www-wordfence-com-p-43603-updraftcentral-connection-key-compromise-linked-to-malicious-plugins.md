Wordfence reports that TeamUpdraft disclosed unauthorized access to its teamupdraft.com website during the October 17–18, 2026 weekend. The incident involved a suspected customer-database compromise and was linked to malicious plugin installation on WordPress sites.

TeamUpdraft says the database contained connection keys for UpdraftCentral, UpdraftVault, and UpdraftClone, as well as customer account information. Those keys are the central security concern for operators using the connected services.

The incident remains in progress in the supplied classification. The evidence does not include technical attack details, indicators of compromise, affected-site scope, mitigation steps, or remediation guidance, so operators cannot derive a complete response procedure from this material alone.
