WP Tavern’s Jukebox Podcast describes Jetpack’s new podcasting features for WordPress sites. Automattic representatives Rob Pugh and Tony Arcangelini say the functionality evolved from WordPress.com’s basic podcasting tools and is now available through Jetpack.

According to the discussion, creators configure show settings, assign a designated category, and add audio or video to a regular WordPress post. Jetpack distinguishes qualifying posts from ordinary content and publishes them through a separate podcast RSS feed. The offering also includes podcast-focused blocks and connections to stats, newsletters, podcast apps, and Jetpack AI.

The episode explains product workflow and rationale, but does not provide detailed implementation guidance, benchmarks, pricing, or adoption evidence.
