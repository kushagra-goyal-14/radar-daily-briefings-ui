Google has announced Gemini 4 Argon, a frontier model currently rolling out to trusted cyber defenders through the Fairwind Program. The company plans broader access after additional testing and guardrail work.

Google says Argon expands the output limit to 1 million tokens and targets long-horizon software engineering, enterprise knowledge work, and cybersecurity defense. The announcement describes agent-assisted C/C++ to Rust migrations, vulnerability remediation, and multiple benchmark results.

The release remains a proposal-stage company announcement rather than broad availability. Reported performance, safety, and migration outcomes are company claims, and the supplied material does not include independent validation or detailed benchmark methodology.
