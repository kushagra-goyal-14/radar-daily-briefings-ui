Google has announced Gemini 4 Argon as an experimental frontier model, initially rolling it out to trusted cyber defenders through the Fairwind Program. Broader access for developers, enterprises, and consumers is planned after further testing and guardrail work.

Google says Argon supports a 1-million-token output limit and handles long-horizon software engineering, enterprise knowledge work, and cybersecurity defense. The company describes agents performing large-scale C/C++ to Rust migrations and autonomously finding, validating, and patching vulnerabilities.

Google reports strong results on DeepSWE v1.1 and CWE-bench v1, alongside internal engineering examples. These claims come from the announcing company, and the supplied evidence provides no independent methodology or validation.
