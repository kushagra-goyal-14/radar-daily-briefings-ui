Simon Willison’s September 2026 blog post, “S3 Is the Future, S3 Is the Past,” catalogs Amazon S3 storage prices from 2006 onward. It observes that the listed rate remains $0.023 per GB-month, unchanged since December 2016.

The post presents a short historical series: earlier reductions brought the rate from $0.150 per GB-month in 2006 to $0.023 in 2016, with no further reduction listed through 2026.

For engineers, the series is useful context when reviewing long-term storage-cost assumptions. However, the supplied evidence does not analyze pricing drivers, other S3 charges, or architectural alternatives.
