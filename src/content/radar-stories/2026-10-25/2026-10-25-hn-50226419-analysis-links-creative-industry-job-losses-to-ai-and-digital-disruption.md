Joseph Politano’s analysis reports that U.S. creative industries lost more than 200,000 jobs over four years, including more than 100,000 in movie and sound recording. It presents the period as an unusually severe downturn outside a generalized recession.

The article argues that AI may be contributing to the decline through inexpensive generation of digital media and competition from AI-assisted content. It also identifies consolidation, offshoring, streaming economics, and social media as competing explanations.

The analysis compares today’s transition with earlier disruptions in music, publishing, and video. Its central caveat is that AI’s causal share is difficult to measure, so the reported employment changes should not be treated as an independently established AI effect.
