An official WordPress Campus Connect post welcomes students from Presidency University, Dhaka, and outlines ways to remain involved after the workshop. It is onboarding guidance rather than a documented software release.

Participants are encouraged to comment on the post, join the WordPress Campus Connect Slack community, and introduce themselves. The post also describes a WordPress.com Education offer providing one free year of hosting and a .blog or .art domain.

Students can additionally suggest Bengali translations for WooCommerce or Rank Math SEO strings through WordPress.org. Translation suggestions may require reviewer approval, and the supplied material reports no participation or outcome metrics.
