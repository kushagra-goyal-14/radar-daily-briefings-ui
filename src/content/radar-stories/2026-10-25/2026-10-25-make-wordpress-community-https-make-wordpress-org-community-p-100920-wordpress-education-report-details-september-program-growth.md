WordPress’s September 2026 education report describes activity across Campus Connect, WordPress Credits, Facilitator Training, and Student Clubs. It reports 13 Campus Connect events during the month and expanded Credits participation across multiple countries.

The report says WordPress Credits had 45 partner institutions, 363 graduates, 522 sites created, and 459 first contributions as of the October 1 snapshot. Its Foundations course now uses autograding, while Designer and Developer tracks are live or being piloted.

The report also documents mentor pathways, faculty workshops, and a proposed Student Clubs lifecycle. Metrics are community-reported, and proposed or pilot initiatives should be distinguished from live programs.
