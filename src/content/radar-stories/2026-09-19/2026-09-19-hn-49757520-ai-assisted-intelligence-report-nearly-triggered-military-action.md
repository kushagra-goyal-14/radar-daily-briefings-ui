CNN reports that a US military analyst used a chatbot to assess intelligence about a Chinese ship’s manifest. The chatbot inaccurately identified the cargo, and the resulting report nearly prompted plans to intercept the vessel during the war with Iran.

The report says the chatbot fused open-source intelligence with secret signals intelligence, then helped package the conclusion into a standard intelligence report. The report does not identify the tool or the cargo it misidentified.

The episode illustrates how unverified AI output can enter high-stakes workflows. CNN also reports decentralized military AI deployment and no single standard for verifying generated information, while relevant commands did not respond to requests for comment.
