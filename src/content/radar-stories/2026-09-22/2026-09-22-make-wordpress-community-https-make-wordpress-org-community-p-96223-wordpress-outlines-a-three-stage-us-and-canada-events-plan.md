A WordPress community post outlines a three-stage plan for events in the United States and Canada through 2027. The first stage, restoring meetups, is underway through the end of 2026; later stages propose more local events and retaining formats that work.

The plan offers a menu including local WordCamps, contributor days, do_action hackathons, developer days, Campus Connect, and one-day events. Support may include venue discussions, outreach, budgeting, sponsorship, and logistics.

The proposal emphasizes activities where participants contribute or build together. It projects twenty or more events, but that outcome is not established, and most participating communities and dates remain unspecified.
