xAI has released Grok 4.7 for coding and knowledge work. The company says it is available through Cursor, Grok Build, the Grok API, third-party coding harnesses, model routers, and cloud platforms.

According to xAI, Grok 4.7 uses a larger base model and a longer reinforcement-learning run focused on difficult, multi-hour tasks. The company says it improves self-verification, long-context handling, and understanding of the Grok Bot harness. Pricing starts at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens.

The announcement also presents benchmark and safety results, including jailbreak-resistance claims. Methodology and independent validation are not supplied, so engineers should treat those comparisons as vendor-reported evidence.
