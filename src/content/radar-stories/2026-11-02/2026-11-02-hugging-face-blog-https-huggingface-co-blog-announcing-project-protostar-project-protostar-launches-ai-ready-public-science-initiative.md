Hugging Face and partner organizations have announced Project ProtoStar, an initiative to make publicly funded scientific datasets, models, and code easier to discover, reuse, and combine. The initiative is proposed through institutional commitments and open infrastructure rather than a completed implementation.

ProtoStar promotes AI-ready, machine-actionable formats, documentation, metadata, and provenance. It will also organize open challenges to demonstrate scientific reuse and identify interoperability, documentation, and technical barriers.

The first challenge, hosted by Carnegie Mellon University from November 2–13, 2026, asks teams to forecast four urban climate indicators for 210 U.S. cities and compare results with simple statistical baselines. Final results are planned for early 2027.
