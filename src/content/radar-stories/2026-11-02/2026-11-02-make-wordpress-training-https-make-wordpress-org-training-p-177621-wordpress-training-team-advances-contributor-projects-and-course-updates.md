The WordPress Training Team’s October 27 recap reports several active initiatives, including proposed group contribution projects, learning-pathway reviews, issue cleanup, and a revised 50-hour contribution course launched on the Education Platform.

The proposed group-project workflow uses a coordinator, a GitHub issue defining scope and a four-to-six-week target, newcomer-friendly tasks paired with experienced contributors, regular status updates, and a closing retrospective. The team also listed queues for content review, feedback validation, topic vetting, and fixes.

Several efforts remain in progress or seek feedback, including handbook updates, translation reviews, a homepage prototype, and text-to-speech prototypes. The recap does not provide outcomes for these activities.
