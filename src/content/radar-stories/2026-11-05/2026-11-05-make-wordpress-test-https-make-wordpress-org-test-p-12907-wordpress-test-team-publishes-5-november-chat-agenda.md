The WordPress Test Team has published an agenda for its 5 November voice chat, scheduled for 18:00 GMT+3 in the #core-test Slack channel. The agenda invites contributors to attend and seeks volunteers for the next facilitator and note-taker roles.

Planned discussions include a Weekly Testing Digest workflow failure involving a Trac scrape timeout, proposed Test Team profile badge criteria, a Contributor Toolkit testing guide, and a new Trac need-testing announcer.

The agenda also highlights WordPress 7.2 beta testing, Gutenberg issues, Xdebug support in the Contributor Toolkit, and DataForm inspector testing. It records planned coordination, not completed work or meeting outcomes.
