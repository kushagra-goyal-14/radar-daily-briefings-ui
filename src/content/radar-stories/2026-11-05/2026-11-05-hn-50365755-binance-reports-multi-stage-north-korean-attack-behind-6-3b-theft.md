Binance reported that a sophisticated, multi-stage campaign preceded an October 29 cryptocurrency theft reported at $6.3 billion. The exchange said the campaign was not a single phishing incident and involved fake job interviews, malware, compromised internal AI tooling, endpoint takeover, and an advanced transaction-authorization method.

According to Binance, malware-laced code replaced a legitimate pyenv component on an engineer’s work computer. Attackers later used the compromised systems, stolen credentials, and a container deployment permission token to deploy a container and trigger the theft.

Binance said it rebuilt the wallet system and internal AI tool, replaced affected devices, and decommissioned credentials and tokens. Its investigation and independent security review were ongoing.
