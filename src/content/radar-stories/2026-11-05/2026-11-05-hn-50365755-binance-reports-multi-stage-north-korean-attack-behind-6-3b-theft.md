Binance reports that a sophisticated, multi-stage campaign preceded the October 29 theft reportedly valued at $6.3 billion. The exchange says the campaign began with social engineering through a fake job interview and a malware-laced coding assignment.

According to Binance, the malware replaced a legitimate pyenv component, executed commands, and communicated with an attacker-controlled server. Attackers later used the compromised endpoint, an internal AI tool, stolen credentials, and a container deployment permission token to reach wallet infrastructure and trigger the theft.

Binance says it rebuilt affected systems, replaced devices, and decommissioned credentials and tokens. The supplied evidence is a secondary report and does not independently verify attribution or recovery outcomes.
