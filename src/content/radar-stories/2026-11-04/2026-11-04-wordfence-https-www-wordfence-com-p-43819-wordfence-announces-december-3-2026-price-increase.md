Wordfence has announced that pricing for Wordfence Premium, Care, and Response will increase on December 3, 2026. The notice is presented as advance guidance for customers planning purchases or renewals.

Wordfence says customers can purchase new licenses or renew eligible subscriptions at current pricing before the deadline. Existing licenses may be renewed up to one year before their expiration dates.

The announcement is relevant to organizations budgeting for Wordfence services, but it does not state the size of the increase or describe any accompanying technical or service changes.
