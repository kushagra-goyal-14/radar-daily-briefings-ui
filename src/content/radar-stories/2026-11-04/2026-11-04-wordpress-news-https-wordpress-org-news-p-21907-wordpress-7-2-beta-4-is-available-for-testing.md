WordPress 7.2 Beta 4 is available for download and testing as an incremental milestone toward the scheduled December 8, 2026 final release. The announcement explicitly limits it to test environments and local sites, not production or mission-critical websites.

The beta refines accessibility, editor reliability, developer APIs, compatibility, and the Ipsum default theme. It also improves translation update management by showing individual available language updates on the Updates screen for selective installation. Developers can test through the Beta Tester plugin, direct downloads, WP-CLI, or WordPress Playground.

The release remains experimental. The announcement provides limited implementation detail, so compatibility and behavior should be validated in isolated environments before reporting issues or planning upgrades.
