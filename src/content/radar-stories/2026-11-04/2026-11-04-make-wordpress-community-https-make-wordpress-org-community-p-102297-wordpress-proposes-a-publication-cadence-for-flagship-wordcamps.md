A WordPress community proposal recommends a standard publication cadence for flagship WordCamps on WordPress.org/news. It is intended to guide 2027 event coverage and remains a proposal.

The framework calls for announcements about organizers and sponsors, ticket sales, scholarships, schedules, and post-event recaps at specified intervals. It is based on a comparison of 2026 coverage for WordCamp Asia, WordCamp Europe, and WordCamp US, along with observed event-site traffic.

The document says traffic to us.WordCamp.org doubled in 2026 compared with 2025 or either sibling event, but supplies no rigorous measurement methodology. Organizers can use the schedule as planning guidance rather than established evidence of promotional effectiveness.
