A WordPress community proposal recommends a consistent publishing schedule for 2027 flagship WordCamps. It remains a proposal and asks for future coordination around event-news publication.

The suggested cadence calls for posts about organizers and sponsors, ticket sales, scholarships, schedules, and comprehensive recaps. The author points to existing WordPress.org/news examples and says those posts support newsletters, social amplification, and event-site visits.

The author reports that us.WordCamp.org traffic doubled in 2026 compared with 2025 or either sibling event, but the supplied document gives limited methodology for that comparison. The framework is intended for WordCamp organizers and community communicators.
