Google has accepted a donation of gVisor, including its name and trademarks, to the Cloud Native Computing Foundation. The CNCF accepted the application on September 28, 2026, and the announcement was published on October 2.

The project plans to move toward CNCF Sandbox status, migrate build and testing infrastructure to GitHub Actions and Buildkite, and adopt a maintainers-based governance model with non-Google merge permissions. Longer-term plans include organization-based voting and possible CNCF Incubation.

The announcement says the transition aims to broaden contribution and integration around gVisor’s container sandboxing role. Several changes remain planned, and the supplied evidence does not independently verify performance or adoption claims.
