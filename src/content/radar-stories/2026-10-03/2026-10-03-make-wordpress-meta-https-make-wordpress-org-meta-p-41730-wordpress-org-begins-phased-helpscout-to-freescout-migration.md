WordPress.org Systems has provisioned a self-hosted FreeScout instance and is planning a phased migration of HelpScout inboxes over the coming weeks. The post describes approximately 20 mailboxes and 86 accounts affected.

Migration phases are organized by mailbox size, automation, sensitivity, traffic, and customization. The plan includes replicating workflows, custom fields, saved replies, external API intake, webhooks, and other integrations. FreeScout access will use WordPress.org SSO, with synchronized account information and Two Factor authentication.

The schedule remains subject to change based on Systems team availability and workflow-replication effort. The supplied evidence documents an in-progress plan, not completed migrations or a final completion date.
