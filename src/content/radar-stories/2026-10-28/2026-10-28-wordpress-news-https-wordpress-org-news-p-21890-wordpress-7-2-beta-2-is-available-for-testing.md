WordPress 7.2 Beta 2 is available for download and testing. The release builds on Beta 1 with enhancements, bug fixes, and refinements across administration and developer APIs; the final release is scheduled for December 8, 2026.

Development continues on the Abilities API, including content querying, capability requirements, schema handling, and permissions. WordPress also adds server-side registration capabilities for DataViews and DataForm, plus REST API, admin asset-loading, and SVG icon improvements.

Developers can test through the Beta Tester plugin, WP-CLI, direct download, or WordPress Playground. The beta is not suitable for production or mission-critical sites, and the supplied material does not detail implementation measurements.
