Learn WordPress is inviting experienced WordPress users, contributors, developers, designers, educators, and community members to review its three existing Learning Pathways: User, Developer, and Designer. The initiative is currently a request for feedback.

Reviewers are asked to identify outdated instructions, software references, tools, workflows, missing concepts, and issues with clarity or structure. Feedback can concern a single lesson and should include the pathway, lesson URL, issue, suggested improvement, and supporting references when available.

Learn WordPress says it will group suggestions, prepare draft improvements, and involve subject-matter experts in checking technical accuracy and priorities. The supplied material does not include completed changes or a timeline.
