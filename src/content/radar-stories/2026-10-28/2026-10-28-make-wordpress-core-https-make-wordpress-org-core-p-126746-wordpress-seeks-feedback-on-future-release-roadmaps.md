WordPress is asking the community to help shape future release-roadmap posts. The request is open to core committers, extenders, hosting companies, and everyday users, with feedback requested by November 16.

The post says roadmap entries have used roughly the same format and approach for the past three years, with variation based on how mature work areas are. Participants can use the linked survey or comment directly if their feedback does not fit the survey format.

After the deadline, the author plans to summarize the feedback in a separate post or comment. No specific technical change or revised roadmap format has been defined yet.
