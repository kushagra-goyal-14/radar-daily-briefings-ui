Liquid AI has released LFM3, an open-weight family of compact multimodal models. It includes text, vision, audio, omni, and decision variants built from the LFM3-1.7B-Base backbone.

The architecture combines gated short convolutions, sliding-window attention, and global attention. Liquid AI also describes hardware-in-the-loop architecture search across phones, Raspberry Pi, laptops, cloud systems, and Qualcomm hardware. The text model supports a 256K context window, while vision, audio, and omni variants add video, speech, and streaming capabilities.

The models are positioned for local agents, specialized fine-tuning, edge inference, and robotics. Reported benchmarks and performance figures come from Liquid AI and are not independently validated in the supplied evidence.
