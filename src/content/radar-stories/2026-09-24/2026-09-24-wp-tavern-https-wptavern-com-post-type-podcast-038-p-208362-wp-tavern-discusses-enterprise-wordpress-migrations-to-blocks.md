WP Tavern’s podcast features K. Adam White discussing enterprise migrations into WordPress blocks. The conversation covers moving tens or hundreds of thousands of posts from systems such as Sitecore and from other WordPress page builders.

White describes using theme patterns as structural templates, inserting source data into pattern HTML, and saving the result as post content. The workflow combines source-content auditing, migrator development, stakeholder spot checks, and validation of missing output types.

Human Made’s rehydrator is presented as a very early-stage open-source experiment. The discussion also identifies editorial review and granular content permissions as recurring WordPress gaps; no release status or independent performance evidence is supplied.
