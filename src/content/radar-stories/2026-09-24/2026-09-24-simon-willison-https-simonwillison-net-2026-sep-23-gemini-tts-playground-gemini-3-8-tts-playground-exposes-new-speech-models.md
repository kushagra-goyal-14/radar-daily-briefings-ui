Simon Willison’s Gemini 3.8 TTS Playground provides a browser interface for testing Google’s newly released Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Flash-Lite TTS models. It supports narration, multi-speaker conversations, audio previews, and bookmarkable settings.

The article says the models offer more than 2,000 voices and can create a custom voice from a 30-second sample or an authorized voice. The playground also exposes request and response details and uses a bring-your-own-key workflow.

For one example, Willison reports generating 1 minute 18 seconds of audio in about 20 seconds for 2.74 cents using Flash TTS. That observation is not a general performance benchmark.
