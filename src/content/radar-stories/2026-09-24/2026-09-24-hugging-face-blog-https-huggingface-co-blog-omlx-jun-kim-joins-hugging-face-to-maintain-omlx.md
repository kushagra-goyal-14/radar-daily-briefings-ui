Hugging Face says Jun Kim, creator and maintainer of oMLX, has joined the company and will continue leading the project. The announcement presents this as a move from side-project maintenance to a funded, fully maintained effort.

Hugging Face says oMLX will remain Apache 2.0. It describes the project as a potential testbed for new ideas while building on mlx-lm and mlx-vlm, with a focus on turning Transformers model definitions into reference MLX implementations for different engines.

The announcement is significant for developers building local AI on Apple Silicon, but it provides no architecture details, performance measurements, or implementation timeline.
