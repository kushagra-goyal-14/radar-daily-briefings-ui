Anthropic reports that Claude autonomously identified a previously uncharacterized enzyme system, called array-associated reverse transcriptases (ARTs), during a large-scale search of DNA sequence data. The company says human scientists subsequently analyzed and tested the candidate in its laboratory.

ARTs are found mainly in bacteriophages and combine a reverse transcriptase, an adjacent partner gene, and a long array of evenly spaced DNA repeats. Anthropic reports that the array is expressed as distinct short RNAs, resembling one feature of CRISPR systems.

The work remains in progress: ART’s primary function is unknown and further experiments are underway. The supplied material also lacks full reproducibility details for the multi-agent search and validation process.
