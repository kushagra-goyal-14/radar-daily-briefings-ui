WordPress is proposing a shift from in-person release events to livestreamed Zoom webinars, following experiments at State of the Word 2025 and WordPress 7.1 at WordCamp US. The new format is planned to begin with WordPress 7.2 in early December 2026.

The webinar would let release-squad members and others closely involved in a release share updates, while viewers watch and ask questions. The proposal says this should reduce event-related logistics, financial burdens, and fixed-date constraints, while enabling broader participation where timezones allow.

This remains a release-process proposal. Joining instructions and other operational details are expected closer to the release date.
