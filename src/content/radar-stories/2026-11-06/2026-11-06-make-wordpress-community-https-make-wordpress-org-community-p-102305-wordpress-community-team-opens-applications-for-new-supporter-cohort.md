The WordPress Community Team is checking its current Supporters and Managers while opening applications for a new Event Supporter and Program Supporter cohort. Applications close on 18 November 2026.

Event Supporters mentor organizers, review local community applications, and monitor dormant communities. Program Supporters review applications, orient lead organizers, and support the Community Team inbox. Both roles involve sensitive information and require a contributor and confidentiality agreement, with role-specific experience, training, or references.

Accepted applicants are scheduled for a December welcome session, followed by role- and region-based orientations with assigned mentors. Current members can remain active, take a break, or step back through the check-in process.
