Simon Willison’s release post covers llm-gemini 0.36, an LLM plugin for Google’s Gemini family. It reports that Gemini 4 Carbon is now available beyond a previously described subset of cyber defenders.

The post lists launch pricing of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with a scheduled increase on December 1. It also describes low, medium, and high thinking efforts, with medium as the default.

Willison demonstrates the model through Antigravity IDE, including a Three.js animation using OpenStreetMap data. The supplied evidence provides examples rather than systematic benchmarks, and the pricing change is not independently confirmed.
