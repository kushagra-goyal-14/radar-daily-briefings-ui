The WordPress Polyglots team published an agenda for a text chat scheduled for November 11, 2026, at 7:00 GMT in the #polyglots Slack channel. The post invites additions to the agenda.

Topics include WordPress 7.2 Beta 5, a reworked list of style guides, and a call for testing the Badge Designer, described as a simpler way to make WordCamp badges. It also seeks Polyglots meeting facilitators.

The agenda is primarily a coordination document for localization contributors. It provides participation opportunities but does not report implementation details, test results, decisions, or the meeting's eventual outcome.
