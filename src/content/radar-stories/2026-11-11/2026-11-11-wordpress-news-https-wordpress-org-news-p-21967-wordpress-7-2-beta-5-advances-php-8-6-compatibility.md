WordPress 7.2 Beta 5 is available for download and testing as an experimental pre-release. The project provides testing paths through the WordPress Beta Tester plugin, direct download, WP-CLI, and WordPress Playground, and schedules the final release for December 8, 2026.

The beta includes security, accessibility, compatibility, and automatic-update refinements. Changes include application-password creation notifications, improved admin-toolbar keyboard navigation, Visual History accessibility work, Notes permissions fixes, MP3 upload compatibility improvements, and further refinements to the Ipsum theme.

The project reports that all known WordPress compatibility issues with PHP 8.6 are resolved, but plugins and themes still require verification. The beta should not be used on production or mission-critical sites.
