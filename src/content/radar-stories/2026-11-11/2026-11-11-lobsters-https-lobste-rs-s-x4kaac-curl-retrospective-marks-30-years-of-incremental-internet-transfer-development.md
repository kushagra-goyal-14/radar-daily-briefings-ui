Daniel Stenberg’s retrospective marks thirty years since Rafael Sagula published httpget 0.1 in November 1996. It follows the project’s evolution into curl, including the 1998 rename and decades of continued maintenance.

The account describes incremental expansion from IPv4-only HTTP downloads to hostname resolution, Gopher, FTP, uploads, libcurl, and support for changing protocols and platforms. Stenberg emphasizes refactoring rather than rewrites, portability, and preserving APIs and ABIs.

For engineers, the history illustrates how foundational network software can grow through iterative changes and open-source collaboration. Adoption and installation figures in the article are estimates, and the piece provides historical context rather than new implementation guidance.
