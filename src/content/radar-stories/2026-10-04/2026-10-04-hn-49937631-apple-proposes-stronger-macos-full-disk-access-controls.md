Apple has announced plans to introduce additional controls for Full Disk Access in macOS. The company says users will need to take more explicit action before granting applications this broad permission.

Apple describes Full Disk Access as largely bypassing protections that help backup applications function. It says misuse can expose files, mail, messages, and browsing history, and can also compromise the privacy of people communicating with the user.

The announcement links the planned changes to risks from increasingly capable, autonomous AI agents. It does not specify APIs, control design, rollout timing, or migration guidance, so engineers should treat this as a policy direction rather than an implemented change.
