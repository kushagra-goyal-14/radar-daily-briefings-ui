Jamie Tanna recommends Renovate as his preferred tool for keeping dependencies up to date, drawing on approximately five years of use across hosted and self-hosted deployments. The article is an experience-based recommendation rather than a release announcement.

It highlights shareable configuration presets, automatic ecosystem detection, grouped updates, one-off command-line runs, custom managers, and the Dependency Dashboard. These features can coordinate update policies across repositories while accommodating repository-specific configuration.
