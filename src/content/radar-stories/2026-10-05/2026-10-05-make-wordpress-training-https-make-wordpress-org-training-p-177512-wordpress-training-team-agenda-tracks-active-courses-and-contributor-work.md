The WordPress Training Team’s October 6 agenda reports ongoing work across its handbook, courses, lesson thumbnails, and issue-triage process. It also records current review queues and volunteer needs.

The team is updating the handbook, developing the revived Intermediate Plugin Developer Course, and organizing issues into categories for systematic review. The agenda lists 9 issues ready for review, 39 awaiting validation, 2 topics awaiting vetting, and 26 validated items awaiting fixes.

The AI Literacy through WordPress course is live on Learn WordPress, while a Plugin Development Course cohort is planned for October. Office Hours dates remain proposed rather than confirmed.
