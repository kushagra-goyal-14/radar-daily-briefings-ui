The WordPress Training Team reports a completed German-language “Starten mit WordPress” cohort and ongoing work across courses, handbook maintenance, multilingual content, and contributor coordination. The update also links to new AI-focused learning resources.

The DACH cohort ran for seven weeks with seven live sessions, two facilitators per session, and a prepared curriculum. The team is updating outdated handbook material and reorganizing translated content while actively testing a multilingual plugin.

September activity included 17 closed feedback tasks, 22 translated-content tasks, and 14 translations in progress. The update supplies operational counts but little technical detail about implementation or outcomes.
