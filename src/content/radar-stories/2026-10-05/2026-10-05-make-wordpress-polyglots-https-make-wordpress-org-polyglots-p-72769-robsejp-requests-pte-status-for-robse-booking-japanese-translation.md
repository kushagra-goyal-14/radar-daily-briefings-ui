Robsejp has requested Project Translation Editor status for the Japanese translation of the Robse Booking WordPress plugin. The post says 785 strings were imported and are awaiting review or validation.

The request is intended to let the contributor keep the Japanese translation up to date. Robsejp says they already hold Project Translation Editor status for Robse Form Guard and Robse Auto Alpha Slug.

Four warnings remain in the translation, which Robsejp describes as intentional: spacing around separator characters and the formatting of “yen (tax incl.)” in Japanese. The supplied evidence does not indicate whether the new editor request has been approved.
