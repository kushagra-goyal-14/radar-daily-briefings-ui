A federal appeals court in Washington, D.C., upheld the Pentagon’s designation of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk. The decision rejects Anthropic’s challenge to the Department of Defense’s ban on its Claude models.

The designation prevents the U.S. military from using Anthropic’s models and blocks defense contractors from using them in work with the agency. The majority said continued integration of Claude into Defense Department information systems presented a statutory national-security risk.

The panel delayed immediate enforcement while Anthropic considers rehearing or further review. A separate federal court has ruled that a parallel designation was unlawful, leaving the broader dispute unresolved.
