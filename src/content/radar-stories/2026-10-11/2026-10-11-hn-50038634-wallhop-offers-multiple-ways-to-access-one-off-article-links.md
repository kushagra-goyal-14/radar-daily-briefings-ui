WallHop presents itself as a replacement for 12ft.io and provides several ways to process article links without a signup. The site documents an iPhone Shortcut, a URL-prefix method, a JavaScript bookmarklet, and raw and API endpoints.

Users can invoke the Shortcut from an article’s Share button, prefix a link with a slash, drag the bookmarklet to a bookmarks bar, or use `/raw/` and `/api/` URL forms. The supplied material does not explain the underlying implementation.

WallHop is positioned for one-off articles from sites users do not regularly visit. It encourages subscriptions for publications read every day, while leaving compatibility, constraints, and technical comparison with 12ft.io undocumented.
