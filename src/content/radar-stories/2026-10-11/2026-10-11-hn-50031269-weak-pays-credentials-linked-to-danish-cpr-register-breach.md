At least three accounts at Pays ApS, including an administrator account, reportedly used the password “123456” when attackers gained access to Denmark’s CPR register. Pays confirmed that its authorized access had been abused.

The CPR register contains personal information about people living or previously registered in Denmark. The breach reportedly exposed information associated with around 8.8 million CPR numbers, while access continued for 21 days and 17 hours.

The supplied report says an anonymous hacker initially used a leaked former employee password and created programs to retrieve data externally. These details remain partly unverified, and the evidence does not establish whether the information was published or misused.
