The WordPress Training Team’s November 10, 2026 agenda documents ongoing work across Learn WordPress, including learning-pathway reviews, translation feedback, handbook updates, and issue triage. It also lists volunteer opportunities and contribution queues.

The team is discussing text-to-speech prototypes for course lessons, a block-based homepage prototype, and a workflow for categorizing and reviewing open issues. The agenda reports nine issues ready for review, 37 awaiting validation, two awaiting topic vetting, and 27 awaiting fixes.

A new version of WordPress Credits: Intro to Contribution has launched on the Education Platform, where mentors can track student progress. The agenda reports more than 50 participating institutions, but supplies limited outcome detail.
