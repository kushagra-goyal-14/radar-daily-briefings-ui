WordPress Credits has expanded to more than 50 educational institutions, up from two in September 2025 and 27 in June 2026. The program connects students with experienced contributors through academic participation.

Students may translate WordPress, improve documentation, create learning materials, or contribute to development and testing. The program says its dashboard recorded institutions in 17 countries, 364 graduates, and contributions across 18 WordPress teams as of October 19.

Institutions can incorporate the program into courses, internships, or final projects. Participation is free for partner institutions and supports flexible formats, but the article provides limited detail about curriculum and contribution operations.
