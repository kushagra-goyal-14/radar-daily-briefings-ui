NVIDIA and Elith have released Nemotron-Personas-Finance-Japan, a synthetic dataset for Japanese financial AI. It contains 9 million personas across 50 fields and is available under CC BY 4.0 for commercial and non-commercial use.

The dataset combines demographic, labor, household-finance, asset, financial-literacy, and personality context. Its generation stack includes NeMo Data Designer, NeMo SDG-PGMs, and gemma-4-31B-it, with Japanese natural-language attributes and distributions grounded in official statistics and public survey data.

The release targets dialogue generation, customer simulation, bias testing, and domain-specific agents. The article says all personas are synthetic, but the supplied evidence includes no independent validation, benchmark results, or adoption data.
