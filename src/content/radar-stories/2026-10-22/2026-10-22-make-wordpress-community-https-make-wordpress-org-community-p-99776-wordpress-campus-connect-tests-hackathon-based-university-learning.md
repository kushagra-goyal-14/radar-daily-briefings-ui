WordPress Campus Connect reports a three-day experiment at Stamford University Bangladesh that combined two online hackathon days with a seminar and workshop. The organizers report approximately 180 registrations, 100 hackathon registrations, 40 websites created, and 82 WordPress profiles created.

The event used beginner, designer, and developer tracks. Participants researched WordPress, built websites, or created basic plugins; AI tools were allowed in the developer track. The organizers observed active self-directed learning but also difficulty for beginners who lacked immediate support.

Approximately half of registered students did not participate, and judging mixed skill levels remained challenging. The organizers are considering a workshop-plus-challenge-plus-hackathon model, while emphasizing that this first experiment is not a proven approach.
