The WordPress Training Team’s October 20, 2026 recap documents ongoing work across contributor education, content maintenance, and team coordination. It lists active review queues, volunteer requests, course updates, and planned Office Hours.

The team is updating its handbook, reviving the Intermediate Plugin Developer Course, and gathering feedback on text-to-speech prototypes for lessons. The recap also reports that a new version of the 50-hour WordPress Credits: Intro to Contribution course has launched on the Education Platform, where mentors can track student progress.

Follow-up discussion settled the next Office Hours session for October 28 at 15:00 UTC on Zoom. The text-to-speech proposal and issue-cleanup priority remain unresolved areas for contributors.
