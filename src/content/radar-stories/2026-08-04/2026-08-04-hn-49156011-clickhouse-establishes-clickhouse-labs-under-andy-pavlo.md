Andy Pavlo says he is joining ClickHouse to establish and lead ClickHouse Labs, a proposed industry research organization focused on databases. The group is intended to collaborate with ClickHouse engineers, customers, collaborators, and industry partners.

The announcement identifies ClickHouse and PostgreSQL as foundations for studying transactional and analytical workloads. Planned topics include validating existing ClickHouse ideas, new hardware, algorithms, data structures, execution strategies, and how database systems should support or be improved by AI agents.

The document describes research priorities rather than a released product or implementation. It provides no new architecture, measurements, or reproducible methods, and Pavlo says the AI-related questions remain unanswered.
