Baochip is offering pre-orders for the Dabao Evaluation Board, a two-layer development board built around the Baochip-1x RISC-V microcontroller. The campaign is funded, but the product remains a planned shipment rather than an established release.

Baochip says the chip combines mostly open compute RTL, a reproducible open bootloader, IRIS-inspectable silicon, hardware security primitives, and a four-core 700 MHz PicoRV-based BIO I/O coprocessor. The BIO includes dedicated RAM, GPIO, inter-processor communication, and event handling.

The board targets embedded developers and security-focused projects. The campaign says mostly -A0 stepping chips will ship because -A1 may not reach full production until later in 2026, while supply-chain conditions could affect delivery timing.
