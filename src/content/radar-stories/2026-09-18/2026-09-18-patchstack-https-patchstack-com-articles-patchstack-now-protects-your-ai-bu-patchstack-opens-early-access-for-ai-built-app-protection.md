Patchstack has opened Early Access for protecting Lovable, Replit, Base44, Claude Code, and other JavaScript applications. The company presents the offering as an extension of its security approach beyond WordPress.

Patchstack says RapidMitigate runs inside the application, identifies vulnerabilities, traces reachability, and automatically activates mitigation rules at runtime without codebase changes or rebuilds. Its new Live Hardening module filters outgoing responses for exposed secrets, tokens, private keys, and sensitive error messages.

Early Access is free before standard SaaS pricing applies. The announcement supplies no benchmarks, implementation specifics, or independent assessment of coverage and effectiveness.
