The essay argues that browser application development has become substantially more accessible because browsers expose powerful capabilities at relatively low cost. It cites the author's TinyLittleOffice project and Simon Willison's Battle Drones as examples.

The technologies discussed include the Web Crypto API, WebAssembly, WebGL, Web Audio API, JavaScript, HTML, and PDF.js. The essay also highlights multilingual delivery, accessibility infrastructure, and offline operation as web-platform strengths.

For engineers, the piece is a useful survey of browser capabilities and project examples, but it is opinionated and offers little reproducible implementation or performance evidence. Its claims about economic transformation and app-store disruption remain speculative.
