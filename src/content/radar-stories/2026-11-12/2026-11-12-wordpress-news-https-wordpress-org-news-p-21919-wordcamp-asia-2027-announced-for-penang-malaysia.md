WordPress has announced WordCamp Asia 2027 for April 9–11 at the Penang Waterfront Convention Centre in Penang, Malaysia. Tickets are on sale, and sponsorship applications are open.

The event will include three days of contribution, learning, and connection. Contributor Day on April 9 requires separate registration, opens January 27, 2027, and is limited to 600 participants. Regular tickets cost USD $35 and cover both conference days, lunch, refreshments, and the after party.

The announcement provides practical planning information for developers, contributors, agencies, and sponsors. It is primarily a community-event notice and offers no technical implementation or engineering analysis.
