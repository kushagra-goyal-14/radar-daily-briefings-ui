WP Tavern published a podcast episode featuring Tiffany Farriss, interim CEO of the Drupal Association, on sustainability across Drupal, WordPress, and the broader PHP ecosystem.

Farriss describes stewardship work beyond source code, including hosting, packaging, testing, security coordination, and governance. She frames this as a maker-taker problem: a small group may fund infrastructure and operational work used by a much larger community. She also discusses measuring contributions and developing cost-recovery approaches.

The episode is relevant to maintainers and engineering organizations planning long-term support for open-source platforms. Its numerical claims, including contribution concentration and estimated per-site services, come from Farriss and are not independently verified in the supplied material.
