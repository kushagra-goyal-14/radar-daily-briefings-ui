Simon Willison describes October 2026 as a potential inflection point for AI-assisted game development, based on four browser games he built with frontier models. The article is a personal case study, not a formal evaluation.

His projects use HTML and JavaScript, with technologies including three.js and Phaser, and run through static hosting. He reports that Claude Opus 5.5 helped with 3D modeling, music, and game design. Emberwick required playtesting and prompting to fix gameplay issues, while Battle Drones grew into a multiplayer real-time strategy game.

The examples suggest a practical workflow for rapid experimentation, but the evidence remains anecdotal and model comparisons are not systematically measured.
