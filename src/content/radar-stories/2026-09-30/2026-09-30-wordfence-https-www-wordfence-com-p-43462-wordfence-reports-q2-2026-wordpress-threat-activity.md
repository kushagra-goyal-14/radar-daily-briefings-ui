Wordfence has published its Q2 2026 WordPress threat intelligence report. The report covers 2,073 published vulnerabilities, including 182 high threat vulnerabilities, and summarizes attack and infection activity.

Wordfence reports 10.4 billion blocked WAF attacks, 18.2 billion blocked brute-force attacks, and 573,000 infected sites. It also discusses vulnerability classes, attacker activity, and malware trends.

These figures provide aggregate context for WordPress security prioritization, but the supplied excerpt does not describe the report’s methodology or provide reproducible evidence.
