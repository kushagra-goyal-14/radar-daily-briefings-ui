The Technology Innovation Institute has introduced Falcon-ASR, a released 1.6-billion-parameter speech-recognition model focused on Arabic, particularly the Emirati dialect. It is available to try through a Hugging Face demo.

TII says Falcon-ASR reaches a 20.92% average word error rate across six Arabic test sets, versus a 23.17% best published result in its leaderboard snapshot. In an internal Emirati evaluation, it reports 22.73% word error rate and 10.19% character error rate. The model also supports English, French, Spanish, and Portuguese with shared weights and provides word-level timestamps.

The results are company-reported, and API access and native applications remain planned.
