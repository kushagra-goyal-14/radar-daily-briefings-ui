A WordPress community proposal outlines a repeatable framework for including education programs and students in flagship and major events starting with WCAsia 2027. It follows experiments at recent WordCamp events and asks organizers for feedback.

The proposed model includes early bridge contacts, one to three reserved program slots, sponsorship or travel support, and flexibility to confirm student features closer to the event. Possible formats include stage presentations, workshops, and Contributor Day participation.

The proposal also recommends shared metrics covering participation, volunteering, contributions, and continued engagement after three or six months. The framework remains subject to organizer input and has not yet been implemented.
