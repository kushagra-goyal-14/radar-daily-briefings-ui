Gutenberg Times’ Weekend Edition 379 reviews WordPress 7.1.4 security fixes, WordPress 7.2 Beta 4, Gutenberg 24.3, WooCommerce updates, Studio, Playground, and agentic-web work. The article is a roundup rather than a single release announcement.

It reports fixes for upgrade-process and stored-XSS issues in WordPress 7.1.4, while 7.2 Beta 4 continues development of the Abilities API and DataViews. Gutenberg 24.3 adds cross-block formatting, Notes reactions, and improved save-error details. Studio now combines AI-assisted site building with live preview, DESIGN.md persistence, annotations, and MCP connections.

The article also describes proposed agentic-web architecture involving MCP, Abilities, OAuth, and a future integrated experience. Several items remain in development or subject to discussion.
