The WordPress Training Team’s November 7 update reports ongoing work on its handbook, Learn WordPress Learning Pathways, and multilingual plugin testing. The team is also reorganizing translated content to improve the Learn website.

The update says the team held four meetings and its sixth Office Hours session in October. Reported queues included nine content items ready for review, 37 feedback items awaiting validation, 22 translations ready for review, and 20 translated items in progress.

The post identifies Slack, GitHub, and the Training Team blog as participation and coordination channels. It provides workload figures but limited detail on implementation methods or completed outcomes.
