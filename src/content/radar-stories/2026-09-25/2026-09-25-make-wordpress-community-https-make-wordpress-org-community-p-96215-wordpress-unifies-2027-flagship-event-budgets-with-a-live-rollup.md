WordPress has provided four 2027 flagship WordCamp organizing teams with separate budget files connected to a central rollup. The templates are being reviewed by the teams, making the workflow an in-progress first version.

Each file preserves local currency, line items, and working practices while exposing an identical summary tab. The rollup imports actual dollar spending from the accounting ledger, maps budget lines to account names, and uses optional due dates to create a monthly cash calendar across all events.

This gives central oversight of payment timing without requiring teams to re-enter ledger data or change their budgeting process. Results from a full cycle are not yet available.
