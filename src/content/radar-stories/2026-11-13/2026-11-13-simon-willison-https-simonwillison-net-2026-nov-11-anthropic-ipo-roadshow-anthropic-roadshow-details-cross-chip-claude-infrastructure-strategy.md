Anthropic’s IPO roadshow video describes how the company trains and serves Claude across AWS Trainium, Google TPUs, NVIDIA GPUs, and AMD GPUs. Simon Willison reports that the video contains previously unreleased business and infrastructure information.

Anthropic says its multi-chip strategy requires tailored software and inference stacks for each accelerator family. It also says Mythos brought Claude to AMD GPUs in three days, with Claude porting or writing almost all kernels.

The company frames compute multipliers as improvements that expand effective compute and suggests Claude could discover them autonomously. These claims remain vendor-sourced; the supplied material includes no reproducible methodology or official transcript.
