Kinsta has released Vulnerability Protection for every WordPress site on its hosting platform, powered by Patchstack and included with each plan. Kinsta says customers do not need to install, configure, or monitor the protection.

The service checks plugins, themes, and WordPress versions against Patchstack’s vulnerability database. When a site runs an affected version, RapidMitigate applies a site-specific rule at the WordPress application layer to block exploit attempts without changing code. Kinsta says the rule is removed after the vulnerable component is updated or deleted.

Kinsta reports more than 1.5 million blocked attempts across over 68,000 sites. Updates remain necessary because mitigation does not fix vulnerabilities.
