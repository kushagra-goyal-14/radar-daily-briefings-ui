The WordPress Test Team reports updated testing queues for Core and Gutenberg as of 25 September 2026. The update lists 130 Core issues needing reproduction and 137 needing patch testing, alongside 14 and one Gutenberg items respectively.

For the reported two-week comparison, Core patch-testing items increased by 20 to 32, while the corresponding Gutenberg count remained zero. The post also notes that review involves code validation and addresses developers.

Contributors can join Patch Testing Sessions on the second and fourth Thursday of each month at 3:00 pm UTC in #core-test. The update provides logistics and queue counts, but no testing procedures or findings.
