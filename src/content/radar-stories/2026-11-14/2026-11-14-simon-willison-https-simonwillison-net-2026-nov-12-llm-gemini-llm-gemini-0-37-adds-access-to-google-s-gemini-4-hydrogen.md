Simon Willison’s release post describes llm-gemini 0.37 alongside Google’s launch of Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a lower-cost member of the Gemini 4 family. The post presents Hydrogen as available through the plugin’s Gemini model access.

Hydrogen is listed at $1.50 per million input tokens, $0.075 per million cached-input tokens, and $7.50 per million output tokens. Carbon and Argon are listed at higher prices that are scheduled to double on December 1, 2026. Google positions Hydrogen for everyday coding execution and multi-model workflows.

The post also says Hydrogen will be the new free-tier model and power AI features in Google Search. These details come from the supplied blog evidence, with no independent validation or access guidance provided.
