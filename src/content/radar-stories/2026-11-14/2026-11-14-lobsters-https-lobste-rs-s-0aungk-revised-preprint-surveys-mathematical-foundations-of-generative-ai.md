Tianhua Chen’s revised 195-page preprint, [The Little Book of Generative AI Foundations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29713), presents a derivation-oriented introduction to modern generative modeling. The arXiv record identifies version 2 as revised on 8 September 2026.

The book connects PCA and probabilistic PCA with variational autoencoders, diffusion models, normalizing flows, autoregressive factorisations, GANs, Wasserstein GANs, and energy-based models. Its stated goal is to preserve mathematical substance while making relationships among model families more accessible.

The material is positioned as a foundation-building primer for mathematically curious researchers, practitioners, and students. The supplied evidence describes a preprint and does not establish peer review or extensive implementation guidance.
