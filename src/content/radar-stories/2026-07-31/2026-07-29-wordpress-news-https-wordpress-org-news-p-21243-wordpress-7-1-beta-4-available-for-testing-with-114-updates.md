WordPress 7.1 Beta 4 is now available for testing. The release contains 114 updates and fixes since Beta 3, split between 51 editor improvements and 63 core fixes. The project's final release date is August 19, 2026.

The project offers four testing methods: the WordPress Beta Tester plugin, direct zip download, WP-CLI, and WordPress Playground (which requires no local setup). Developers can report bugs in the Alpha/Beta support forum, submit reproducible reports to WordPress Trac, or cross-check against the known-issues list.

According to the announcement, Beta 4 focuses on editor stability. Notes now remain reliably anchored to the passages they reference, and tagged people are displayed more clearly.
