The WordPress Developers Chat agenda schedules a July 28, 2026 meeting at 15:00 UTC in the core channel on Make WordPress Slack. The meeting will cover upcoming releases and include an open-floor section.

The agenda identifies WordPress 7.1 Beta 4 as scheduled for July 29 at 15:00 UTC. It also lists dev notes and topics including client-side media processing, text shadow support, media-library infinite scroll, SVG icons, and block-support updates.

Open tickets remain in the 7.1 milestone, and the agenda says most will be deferred if not merged before the following week's RC1 release. The document records planned discussion and timing, not completed implementation.
