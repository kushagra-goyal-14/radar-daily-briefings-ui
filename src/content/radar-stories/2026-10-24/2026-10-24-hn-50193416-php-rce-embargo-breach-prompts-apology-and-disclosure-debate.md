A Hacker News poster apologized for discussing upcoming PHP remote-code-execution vulnerabilities associated with CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619. They said they were unaware of an embargo and intended to alert people who follow PHP's release cycle.

The poster says the disclosure affected the coordinated-release schedule and created additional workload. Discussion participants dispute whether the poster, upstream contacts, or other parties were responsible for the embargo breach, with some arguing that information had already spread.

For security teams, the thread illustrates the operational risk of sharing vulnerability details without complete disclosure context. It provides no official technical analysis, affected-version guidance, or confirmed remediation information.
