Andrej Karpathy argues that understanding of LLM capability is widening into a sharp funnel, with most people having little exposure, casual users seeing chatbot-like behavior, and a small professional group using frontier-grade systems for complex work. The document is an opinion post, not an official release or confirmed technical report.

Karpathy attributes the divergence to problem scale, uneven capability across domains, and access tiers. He says capability is strongest where tasks are digital, verifiable, and economically valuable, and describes agent activity spanning software, cybersecurity, mathematics, and science.
