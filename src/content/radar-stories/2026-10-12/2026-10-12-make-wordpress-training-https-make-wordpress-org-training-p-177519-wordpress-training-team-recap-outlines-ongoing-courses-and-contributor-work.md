The WordPress Training Team’s October 6 recap records ongoing work across contributor support, course development, and repository maintenance. It reports active review queues and invites contributors to help with content, handbook, thumbnail, and issue-cleanup tasks.

The team says the AI Literacy through WordPress course is live on Learn WordPress and plans a Plugin Development cohort during October. It is also gathering feedback on text-to-speech prototypes for course lessons and a block-based Training Team homepage prototype.

The recap provides contribution pathways and queue counts, but limited implementation detail. The next Office Hours date and the Training Team admin meeting schedule remained unresolved.
