The WordPress Test team has announced dedicated Test Scrubs for the WordPress 7.2 milestone, replacing the usual Future Milestone Tickets session. The release is currently targeted for December 8, 2026.

Beginning with the next scheduled session, scrubs will run on the second and fourth Thursday of each month at 3:00 PM UTC in the #core-test Slack channel. Participants will focus on 7.2 tickets marked Has Patch or Needs Testing, with additional attention to features and regressions as the release reaches Beta and RC.

Anyone can lead a scrub by proposing a time and focus area. The announcement provides participation logistics but no measurement of testing outcomes.
