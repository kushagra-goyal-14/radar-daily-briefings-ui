OpenAI has publicly released a catalogue of 722 mathematical manuscripts organized into 372 result families. The repository includes supporting proof artifacts, source files, build instructions, and selected reasoning summaries.

The collection includes Lean formalizations and a formalization catalogue, but OpenAI says coverage is incomplete. It describes results as being at different stages of verification and warns that some unformalized results could contain issues.

For research engineers, the repository offers materials for inspecting, building, and further checking model-produced mathematics. OpenAI says most results came from an unreleased internal model and that revisions will be preserved as new public versions.
