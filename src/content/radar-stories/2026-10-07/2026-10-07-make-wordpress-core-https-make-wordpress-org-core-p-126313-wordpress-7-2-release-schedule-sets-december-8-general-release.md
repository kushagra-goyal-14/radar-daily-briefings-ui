WordPress has published a proposed schedule for WordPress 7.2, with Beta 1 planned for October 20, 2026, and the general release scheduled for December 8 at 15:00 UTC. The schedule also includes four beta releases, three release candidates, a dry run, and a 24-hour code freeze.

Release parties are planned in Slack’s #core channel, where contributors can assist with release testing during the final weeks. The schedule identifies UTC start times and leaves several coordination roles as TBA.

The timeline helps contributors plan testing and participation, but it does not describe implementation changes or release scope. WordPress notes that last-minute adjustments may occur and will be communicated through the post.
