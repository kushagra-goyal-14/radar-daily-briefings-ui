Mistral has launched a public preview of Mistral Large 4, a 1-trillion-parameter natively multimodal model with 49 billion active parameters. The preview API is available through Mistral Studio, while the company says weights will be released by the end of October 2026.

Mistral describes ML4 as an open-weight hybrid instruct-and-reasoning mixture-of-experts model supporting coding, agentic workflows, cybersecurity, document understanding, and visual grounding. It reports training the model from scratch on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in European datacenters.

Engineers can begin evaluation through the preview API, but deployment decisions should account for the unreleased weights and the announcement’s vendor-reported benchmarks. Mistral says further architecture and post-training details are forthcoming.
