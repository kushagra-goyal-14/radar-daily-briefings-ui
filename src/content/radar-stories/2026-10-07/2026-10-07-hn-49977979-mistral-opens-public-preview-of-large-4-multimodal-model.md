Mistral has launched a public preview of Mistral Large 4, an experimental 1 trillion-parameter multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 49 billion active parameters. The preview API is available through Mistral Studio; Mistral says weights will be released by the end of October 2026.

Mistral reports capabilities spanning coding, tool-using agents, cybersecurity, scientific workflows, finance, legal work, and visual grounding. It says the model was trained on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in European datacenters and uses reinforcement learning with large-scale parallel rollouts.

Engineers can test the API now, but the supplied benchmark results are largely vendor-reported. Architecture, additional benchmarks, and post-training details remain forthcoming, and self-hosting cannot yet be verified.
