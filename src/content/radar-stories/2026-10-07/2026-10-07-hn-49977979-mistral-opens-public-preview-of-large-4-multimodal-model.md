Mistral has launched a public preview of Mistral Large 4 through Mistral Studio. The company describes it as a 1-trillion-parameter natively multimodal model with 49 billion active parameters; model weights are planned for release by the end of October 2026.

Mistral says the model was trained on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in its European datacenters. It combines instruction, reasoning, agentic workflows, coding, cybersecurity, and visual understanding, with reported results across several internal and external evaluations.

Engineers can access the preview API to assess relevant workloads before deployment decisions. The evidence is primarily vendor-provided, and additional architecture, benchmark, and post-training details have not yet been published.
