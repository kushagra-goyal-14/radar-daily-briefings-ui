Mistral has launched a public preview of Mistral Large 4, a 1-trillion-parameter multimodal model with 49 billion active parameters. The preview is available through Mistral Studio, while Mistral says the weights are planned for release by the end of October.

Mistral describes the model as an open-weight mixture-of-experts system for coding, cybersecurity, agentic workflows, visual grounding, and scientific tasks. The company reports strong results across DeepSWE, Terminal-Bench, Cybench, and other evaluations, and says it was trained on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in European datacenters.

The preview gives engineers an opportunity to assess API behavior and deployment claims before self-hosting is possible. Benchmark methodology and further architecture details remain forthcoming, and the reported results are largely company-provided.
