The WordPress Training Team’s July 28 meeting recap documents ongoing contributor coordination and proposed work. The team reports about 555 open issues, handbook updates, volunteer recruitment, and an upcoming Office Hours session on August 22, 2026.

The proposed workflow divides issues into smaller categories, starting with potential translation-review work, while contributors also help validate feedback, review content, vet topics, and create lesson thumbnails. A regular biweekly GitHub triage meeting remains under discussion and has not been confirmed.

The recap also reports that Learn WordPress is fully translated into Marathi and announces a Plugin Development cohort. These are community and training updates rather than merged engineering changes.
