Simon Willison reports that OpenAI used an internal version of Astra to pursue ten mathematical problems whose main results had seen no progress for at least a decade. He says the work produced Lean 4 formalizations in the openai/ten-proofs repository, along with a paper and an LLM-generated reconstruction of the proof process.

OpenAI reportedly spent less than $2,000 at GPT-5.6 Sol token prices on each problem. The supplied evidence does not establish how many attempts failed or provide the prompts, system architecture, or evaluation methodology.

The repository and formalizations may support inspection, but the results remain claims reported by a third-party blog in this evidence.
