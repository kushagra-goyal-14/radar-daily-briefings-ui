Andrej Karpathy reports an informal Opus 5 experiment in which he supplied the first paragraph of The Lord of the Rings, a one-million-token budget, and a request for a Three.js rendering. The model worked for about two hours and produced roughly 5,500 lines of code.

Karpathy says the program procedurally arranged polygon assets in three-dimensional coordinates and animated them to render the story. He reports that the model audited the output by taking screenshots at different points, slowly and with several mistakes.

The demonstration suggests potential for highly customized generated worlds, but remains experimental. Its result was visibly rough, and the evidence is neither controlled nor independently verified.
