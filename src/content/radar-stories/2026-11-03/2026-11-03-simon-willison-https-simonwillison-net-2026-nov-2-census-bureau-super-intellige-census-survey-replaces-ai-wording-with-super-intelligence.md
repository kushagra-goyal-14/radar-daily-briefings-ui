The U.S. Census Bureau changed the Business Trends and Outlook Survey’s AI question and later introduced “Super Intelligence” terminology. Simon Willison’s November 2, 2026 article reports that the October 19 collection was the first to use the new wording, while its results were still pending.

Before November 17, 2025, the survey asked about AI used in producing goods or services. It then asked about AI used in any business function, and the current question refers to “Super Intelligence or SI,” formerly AI. The earlier and revised results are not directly comparable.

The evidence does not establish whether the latest terminology changes response rates or reported adoption.
