The WordPress Training Team’s November 3, 2026 agenda coordinates ongoing contributor work across Learn WordPress. It covers learning-pathway reviews, issue triage, volunteer roles, handbook updates, and feedback on a block-based team homepage prototype.

The agenda also describes exploration of text-to-speech functionality for course lessons and reports that a new version of the 50-hour WordPress Credits: Intro to Contribution course has launched within the Education Platform, where mentors can track student progress.

For contributors, the document provides concrete queues and participation paths: 9 issues await review, 37 await validation, 2 await topic vetting, and 27 await fixes. Implementation details for the prototypes and platform integration remain unspecified.
