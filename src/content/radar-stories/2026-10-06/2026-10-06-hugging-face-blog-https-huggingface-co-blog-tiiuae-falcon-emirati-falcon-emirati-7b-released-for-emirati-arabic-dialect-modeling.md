The Technology Innovation Institute has released Falcon-Emirati-7B, a model specialized for understanding and generating Emirati Arabic. It is built on Falcon-H1-Arabic and is available through the Falcon Chat platform.

The reported adaptation combines authentic Emirati web data, MSA-language material about Emirati culture, and synthetic dialect data constrained by Emirati glossaries and style rules. Training experiments varied data mixtures and stages, with native-speaker review complementing automatic metrics.

The article reports 84.83% on Alyah and 85.57% on a UAE cultural-understanding task. These are release-author claims; the model may still produce errors, reflect training-data bias, and require evaluation for sensitive or high-stakes applications.
