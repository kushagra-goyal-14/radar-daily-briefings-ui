The author of Midvash Bible References, a free WordPress plugin, requests review of imported translations for several locales. The translation strings are waiting for review, and the post asks contributors to comment with questions or offers of help.

The listed locales include Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Russian, Korean, and Simplified Chinese. The author requests project translation editor access for Brazilian Portuguese and asks other translators to review strings or take over as project translation editors.

The author says the translations were prepared with AI assistance and have not all been reviewed by native speakers. The post is a contributor request, not evidence that the translations have been approved.
